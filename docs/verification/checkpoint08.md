## Runtime evidence

All compatible checkpoint 08 scenarios use `tests/scenarios/checkpoint08-ability-fixture.js` for the same four-player Match startup. The browser host uses its own fixture because it runs in a separate process. The combined non-browser checkpoint 08 run passed 92 tests, including these comparisons:

- The acquisition matrix grants every covered rarity and rank along a legal path. Chassis matrices compare actual Tail, Slithery, Tentacles, Prehensile Tail, Hollow Bones, More Legs and Fin stats with the recorded tables; separate scenarios check Wings collision, terrain effects, heatwave and blizzard exposure, resistance damage, risk and modules.
- Live attack matrices compare every available rank and rarity for Body Slam (in checkpoint 03), Claws, Toe Beans, Pincer, Jaws, Antlers, Trunk, Horns, Beak, Spit, Projectile Tongue, Leech, Stinger, Stoner's charged and quick throws, and Spur. Leech drain and Stinger poison tick tables are also compared at every rank and rarity. These tests use the recorded midpoint tables and estimates; the source pages are not version-pinned.
- The movement course verifies Dash and Slide against walls and different tick sizes. Slide and Sprint rank/rarity behavior, Leap and Scuttle cooldowns, Leap charge, Bouncy, Heavy landing damage, Arthropod lateral Scuttle, immunity expiry and boundary damage have live checks.
- Specialization scenarios cover both Legs choices, Thrower, Arboreal, Training, Signal, Sharp, Long, Neck Bite, Stalker, Sticky, Whiplash, Anaesthetic, Vasodilators, Marking and Primal. Controlled random draws verify Marking's charm multiplier; Signal tests distinguish owners. Alias tests reject duplicate learned entries; unequipping Beak, Jaws or Trunk retains its salvage benefits.
- The four-client browser journey passed a real Claws offer, Claws damage and Leap movement. The capture is `test-results/checkpoint08/combined-build.webm`; Arms, Legs, Leap and target placement use fixture setup. The screenshot is `test-results/checkpoint08/combined-build.png`.

The exact Body Slam (Tail) identity, Fin values, Arboreal range/cap/decay, Leap timing and Heavy push force remain estimates or unconfirmed as described above. The user authorized informed estimates rather than blocking on exact source confirmation. Live Saliva-to-Spit acquisition remains assigned to checkpoint 09, and live coating integration to checkpoint 10.

The combined foundation and checkpoint 02–08 non-browser regression run passed 235 tests with four skipped. Previously failing checkpoint 02, 03 and foundation fixtures were updated for the current match and selection rules. Foundation's three production-host cases and intentional failure demonstration require special environment settings and were skipped in the ordinary run; they are not counted as passing. The full repository browser suite has not been run.
