# YCCE-TEST-09 source comparison

The table below records an earlier web comparison. Later local configuration imports supersede its Shell, Fibroblasts, and Immune System numbers: Shell's rare resistance is 8/16/25; Fibroblasts uses epic and legendary growth of 1/1.5/2 per 30 and 20 actual hull healed respectively; Immune System resistance is rare 15/30/45, epic 20/40/60, and legendary 25/50/75. The local configuration's game version remains unverified. The current defense scenarios exercise these imported values.

This source comparison covers YCCE-058 through YCCE-061M. The available wiki and trait-index excerpts are not pinned to game version 1.2.1. Direct requests returned 403 during the recorded research. The [content guide](../content/README.md#evidence-limits) records the source selection and implementation estimates. Where the wiki and the trait index conflict, the user selected the wiki value. That decision does not prove either source matches the pinned version.

The comparison below pairs the recorded source mechanics with the current implementation. Rank and rarity tables live in `server/content/starter.ts`; the linked sources and the [source catalogue](../content/source-catalogue.json) retain the evidence and unresolved identity questions. A source comparison is not a runtime result. The tests and recordings listed separately in the checkpoint record provide behavioral evidence.

## Size and stats runtime checks

`node --test tests/scenarios/checkpoint09-stats.test.js` passed ten scenarios after compiling the host with `npx tsc -p tsconfig.host.json`. The shared Match fixture checks every supported rarity and rank for Chonky, More Mass, Elusive, Smol, Muscular Tissue, Agile, and Dexterous against independent expected tables. The same file checks Robust across acquisition orders, Shifty at neutral and size limits, collider radius and mass, growth beside a wall, current versus refilled plating for Overwhelm, seeded dodge, a real Leap cooldown, physical and ability attack damage, and movement during an attack and a stun. These checks demonstrate the implemented formulas, including estimates; they do not resolve the source-version limitations above.

## Resource and defense runtime checks

The shared Match fixture runs the resource, stats, and defense files as one matrix. Its 52 scenarios passed. `checkpoint09-resource.test.js` checks live Saliva acquisition followed by equipped Spit damage, every recorded resource and diet rank table, diet gates in both orders, Cheek Pouch capacity and both specializations, Ruminant timing, and XP queues. A real bot kill produces meat with the killer and owner retained through collection. Controlled comparisons also cover own, allied, and third-party provenance. An independent 2,000 XP grant preserves the sum of spent and remaining XP and one reward per level. A full protected choice still allows passive XP, while boundary damage bypasses protection.

The collection ledger records a Forager repair attempt of 1.3 hull with 0.5 actually restored. Direct repair results show 40, 9, and 2 hull restored across a Fibroblasts threshold; overheal alone adds no maximum hull. Forager reduces a 10-point corrosion tick to 9 while a 10-point boundary hit remains 10. Provider successfully charms a nearby bot under a controlled random draw. Reach extends a live Bop hit without changing salvage reach.

`checkpoint09-defense.test.js` checks the defensive rank tables, Basking on sand, live heatwave adaptation, material exclusions, both Spines choices, both Turtle Down choices, and current versus maximum plating. One contact between Spines owners produces one correctly attributed retaliation, rather than recursion. Impervious splits a 40-point hit into 20 immediate and 20 delayed damage. A later delayed lethal hit produces one death and one credited elimination. A separate disconnected-selection case confirms delayed lethal damage still kills. Early and late guard checks cover a human attacker; a hunter AI initiates its own contact attack in the bot case. Regeneration, healing growth, and an active guard do not prevent collapse. The queued-offer case confirms that resolving a prompt does not refill plating again.

The earlier checkpoint 02 through 08 collision, choice, boundary, and thermal checks are reused. The combined regression run passed 288 tests with four skips. The skips are the foundation production-host cases and intentional failure demonstration, not passing results. The run includes the checkpoint 08 browser scenario; checkpoint 09's browser scenario ran separately.

## Browser journey

Four local browser clients connected to the rebuilt production host. The fixture granted Saliva, Chonky, Carapace, Spines, and rank-three Reach, then placed a common vegetation resource at the player's position. The player collected the resource, earned a level, and selected an offered upgrade through the interface. The test observed the collection ledger and upgrade rank, checked another client could observe the build, and opened the upgrade list with a pointer. The fixture setup is not an organic acquisition of the initial build.

The browser observed the starting attack with Reach and checked the advertised range against the server's attack formula. The client uses that range to draw the cue. The cue is a child of the scaled player mesh, so its local length and position now divide by the parent scale; inspection and the captured frame confirm the cue is drawn. The image is not a pixel measurement of its world-space endpoint. The combined recording is `test-results/checkpoint09/combined-build.webm`; screenshots are `test-results/checkpoint09/combined-build.png` and `test-results/checkpoint09/attack-reach.png`.
